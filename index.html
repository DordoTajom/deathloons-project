About DEATHLOONS

OUR POINT OF VIEW

DEATHLOONS is an independent label and creative house in development, built to connect music, visual identity, and experimental ideas under one direction.

Starting with Dordo Tajom and the KWTFM era, the archive will grow as projects become official. No fake milestones. No noise for the sake of noise. Just work with a world behind it.
